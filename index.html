Index Reader

A minimal static site served from Fly.io.

What this is

A plain static index.html, served by nginx from a small Alpine container. No client-side logic, no external requests, no redirects.

How it is served

Files live at /usr/share/nginx/html. The container listens on port 8080 and Fly terminates TLS in front of it.

Deploy

fly launch --no-deploy then fly deploy, or just fly deploy if the app already exists.
